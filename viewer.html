Words used
FNAC
Fine Needle Aspiration Cytology. The test. A very thin needle takes a few cells from the gland to look at.
Nodule
A lump in the gland. It is a finding, not a diagnosis.
Thyroid
The gland in the front of the neck that controls how the body uses energy.
Numbing injection
A small injection in the skin to make the area less sensitive before the needle.
Passes
Gentle in-and-out movements of the needle to collect enough cells. They last a few seconds.
Repeated test
If the sample is not clear enough to give an answer, the test may need to be done again.
